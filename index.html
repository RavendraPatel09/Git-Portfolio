01 ABOUT

I enjoy learning how software works, building practical projects around what I learn, and continuously experimenting with new technologies.

My engineering path centers on bridging foundational computer science with modern product architectures. Whether designing accessible platforms like Saksham-AI, engineering asynchronous backend services with FastAPI and Node.js, or building real-time WebSockets, I focus on clean structure, type safety, and zero bloat.
